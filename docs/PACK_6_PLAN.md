# Pack 6 plan: The Deep Below

Status: **decided by Claude with the owner's trust** (10/31/2026: "i trust you"; "program all 9 packs 1 at a time";
11/01/2026: "go ahead"). The design (`GAME_DESIGN.md`: Fantasy realms) names each realm, its source and one boss idea;
everything else was chosen by Claude from old public-domain texts only. The owner can change or veto any of it. Nidavellir built and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.90); Svartalfheim (00.02.91); Mycendra (00.02.92). Gullinbursti reuses Morvarc'h's gallop (a golden trail).

Realms work like Pack 5's: endless map, a hazard, monster families, three bosses, no local gods.

## How it is built
- One realm at a time: Nidavellir, Svartalfheim, Mycendra, the Myrmidon Hive. Then two secrets per realm.
- **Heading:** "Pack 6: The Deep Below".
- **Unlock (Claude's choice):** each realm opens from a Pack 5 30:00 boss: the Beast of Gévaudan → Nidavellir, the Wild
  Hunt → Svartalfheim, Carmilla → Mycendra, Black Annis → the Myrmidon Hive.
- **Difficulty:** as Pack 4 and 5 (+60% enemy HP, +35% spawns, +30% damage).
- **Heroes:** none.

## Realm 21: Nidavellir (the dwarven forge-halls): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.90)**
*Sources: the Poetic Edda: Völuspá 37 (the golden hall of Sindri's kin in Niðavellir), Reginsmál (Andvari, his ring and
his curse); Snorri, Skáldskaparmál 35 (Brokkr and Sindri's wager: Gullinbursti, Draupnir, Mjölnir; the fly that stung
Brokkr at the bellows) and 39 (Andvari's gold).* Loki, who is the fly and who robs Andvari, is not a character here.

**Hazard: molten runs.** Bands of molten metal spill across the floor from the forges and burn whoever stands in them
(the heat bands, in metal colours).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dwarf smiths | Chasers with hammers | Völuspá 10-16 (the dwarves); Skáldskaparmál 35 |
| Dwarf hammer-bearers | Brutes | Skáldskaparmál 35 |
| Forge sparks | Bombers (already in the game: Muspell sparks) | Skáldskaparmál 35 (the forge) |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Gullinbursti** | The golden boar Brokkr and Sindri forged, which "runs over air and sea faster than any horse", its bristles lighting the night. It charges across the arena, leaving a trail of golden light that slows |
| 20:00 | **Brokkr** | Every so often he works the bellows while the forge makes a treasure. Hurt him enough while he works ("the fly stings him") and the forging fails; let him finish and his blows grow stronger for a while (the hammer) |
| 30:00 | **Andvari** (the design's) | The dwarf who guarded his gold as a pike in a waterfall. His cursed ring, Andvaranaut, falls in the arena: a Hero who takes it breaks his guard, but is cursed (Hex) |

## Realm 22: Svartalfheim (the dark elves): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.91)**
*Sources: Snorri, Gylfaginning 17 and 34 (the dark elves; Gleipnir made in Svartálfaheimr); the Poetic Edda,
Alvíssmál (the dwarf Alvíss turned to stone by the dawn); Skáldskaparmál 1 (Fjalar and Galar and the mead of poetry);
the Nibelungenlied (c. 1200), 3rd and 8th adventures (Alberich, the cloak of invisibility, the Nibelung hoard).*

**Hazard: the dark below.** The light fails and the view closes in (the weather veil, black).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Svartálfar | Dark elves, casters | Gylfaginning 17 |
| Nibelungs | Alberich's warriors of the hoard, chasers | Nibelungenlied, 3rd adventure |
| Night bats | Swarms and flights (already in the game) | the dark under the earth |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Alvíss** | "All-wise", who answered Thor's questions until the sun rose and turned him to stone. Shafts of daylight fall in the arena; when he stands in one he turns to stone for a while (can't act, takes double damage) |
| 20:00 | **Fjalar and Galar** | The two dwarves who brewed the mead of poetry in the vats Óðrerir, Boðn and Són. Three vats stand in the arena and heal them while they stand; tip a vat over by standing at it |
| 30:00 | **Alberich** (the design's) | The dwarf lord of the Nibelung hoard with his cloak of invisibility (the Tarnkappe). He puts on the cloak and can't be hurt; walk into him to tear it off, as Siegfried did, and he is stunned |

## Realm 23: Mycendra (the fungal city) — invented: **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.92)**
*The design marks this realm invented: no public-domain source was found. Its name, its bosses and its monsters are the
game's own, and are kept as plain descriptive names (no invented proper names), for the owner to approve or rename.
Fungi themselves, fairy rings and puffballs are ordinary nature and folklore.*

**Hazard: spore clouds.** Clouds of spores drift through the city, hiding the view and Poisoning whoever breathes them
(the weather veil, sickly green, with Poison).

| Family | What it does | Name (invented, plain) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walking caps | Mushroom-folk, slow chasers | "Cap walker" |
| Spore puffs | Swarms of drifting puffballs | "Spore puff" |
| Glow flies | Flyers lit by fungus light | "Glow-fly" |

| Time | Boss (invented, plain names) | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Great Puffball** | Every time it loses a tenth of its health it bursts in a cloud of spores that Poisons whoever is near |
| 20:00 | **The Mycelium** | Its threads spread over the ground around it, slowing and Poisoning whoever stands on them; they keep growing while it lives |
| 30:00 | **The Spore Mother** | Mushrooms sprout around the arena and burst into spore clouds after a few seconds; stamp them out first by standing on them |

## Realm 24: the Myrmidon Hive (the ant-warriors)
*Sources: Ovid, Metamorphoses 7.614-660 (Zeus turns the ants of Aegina into the Myrmidons); Herodotus, Histories
3.102-105 (the gold-digging ants of India, smaller than dogs and bigger than foxes, who chase the gold-gatherers);
Servius, commentary on Virgil's Aeneid 4.402 (Myrmex, the Attic girl turned into an ant).*

**Hazard: the tunnels give way.** The hive's tunnels collapse under the ground (the Underworld's chasms, in sand
colours).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Myrmidons | Ant-born warriors, chasers | Ovid, Met. 7 |
| Gold-digging ants | Swarms | Herodotus 3.102 |
| Winged ants | Flyers (the swarming flight) | Herodotus; nature |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Gold-Digging Ant** | It digs up heaps of gold sand. Take the gold (run gold!) and it chases the thief, faster, for a while, as the ants of India chased the gold-gatherers |
| 20:00 | **Myrmex** | The girl who claimed Athena's plough and was turned into an ant. She ploughs furrows across the arena that slow whoever crosses them |
| 30:00 | **The Queen of the Gold-Digging Ants** (the design's) | Worker ants keep coming from the edges, carrying gold to her; each that reaches her heals her. Kill the carriers first |
