# Pack 4 plan: the Elemental Planes

Status: **draft for the owner** (10/31/2026). The owner asked to start Pack 4 once Pack 3 was done ("then start pack
4"). `docs/PACK_PLAN.md` (Phase 33) says the realms must be designed with the owner before any building: the design
names only one boss per realm and no monsters or hazards. This is that design, drafted from the old public-domain texts
only (never modern games, books or films), for the owner to approve, change or reject.

Realms work like the myth levels (endless map, a hazard, monster families, three bosses), but **have no local gods**:
their shrines draw gods from every unlocked pantheon (the Land of Legends rule). Paracelsus' *Liber de Nymphis,
Sylphis, Pygmaeis et Salamandris* (published 1566) gives each element its spirits: salamanders (fire), undines (water),
sylphs (air).

## How it would be built (same as Packs 2 and 3)
- One realm at a time: Muspelheim, Niflheim, Ys, Cloud-Cuckoo-Land.
- **Unlock (suggestion):** each realm opens from a Pack 3 30:00 boss: Louhi → Niflheim (the cold North), the White Div →
  Muspelheim, Koschei → Ys, Shuten-dōji → Cloud-Cuckoo-Land.
- **Difficulty (suggestion):** harder again: against Pack 1, enemy HP +60%, spawns +35%, damage +30%.
- Then, as with Packs 2 and 3: achievements, two secrets per realm, banter, lore check, Resonances if any fit.

## Realm 13: Muspelheim (fire)
*Sources: Snorri, Gylfaginning 4-5, 51 (Muspell, the burning south, guarded by Surtr); the Poetic Edda, Fjölsvinnsmál
(Sinmara, the hall ringed by flame, its hounds and its golden cock); Paracelsus (salamanders).*

**Hazard: falling sparks.** Sparks of Muspell rain down (telegraphed strikes, Fire); in Gylfaginning 5 the gods made
the stars from such sparks.

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Salamanders | Fire-lizards that leave burning ground | Paracelsus |
| Sons of Muspell | Fire-giant brutes | Gylfaginning 51 |
| Muspell sparks | Bombers (already in the game) | Gylfaginning 5 |

| Time | Boss (suggested except Sinmara) | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Gífr and Geri** | Two hounds who guard the gate; one sleeps while the other wakes, and they swap | Fjölsvinnsmál 13-18 (the two hounds, who sleep by turns) |
| 20:00 | **Víðófnir** | The gleaming golden cock on the world-tree; its light blinds, and a dive strikes from above | Fjölsvinnsmál 18-26 |
| 30:00 | **Sinmara** (the design's) | The fire-giantess who keeps the weapon Lævateinn in a locked chest; the arena's flames close in | Fjölsvinnsmál 26-30 |

## Realm 14: Niflheim (ice)
*Sources: Snorri, Gylfaginning 4-5 (Niflheim and the spring Hvergelmir), 16 (the eagle at the world's edge); the
Poetic Edda: Grímnismál 32-35 (Níðhöggr and the serpents under the tree), Völuspá 39 and 44 (Náströnd; Garmr),
Vafþrúðnismál 37 (Hræsvelgr).*

**Hazard: freezing fog.** A white fog closes the view and chills whoever stays in it (the weather veil, with Chill).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Serpents of Hvergelmir | Swarms from the spring | Grímnismál 34 (the serpents named under the tree) |
| Frost thursar | Brutes and casters (already in the game) | Gylfaginning 5 |
| Rime wraiths | Pale flyers of the mist. **Suggested**: the texts give no creature here, only "rime", so the name stays plain | Gylfaginning 5 (the rime of Ginnungagap) |

| Time | Boss (suggested except Níðhöggr) | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Hræsvelgr** | The giant in eagle's shape whose wings make the winds; gusts push Heroes about | Vafþrúðnismál 37 |
| 20:00 | **Garmr** | The hound that howls before Gnipahellir; each howl calls more of the dead | Völuspá 44 |
| 30:00 | **Níðhöggr** (the design's) | The dragon gnawing the world-tree's roots; burrows under and bursts up, roots break the ground | Grímnismál 32-35; Völuspá 39 |

## Realm 15: Ys (water)
*Sources: Albert Le Grand, Life of St Guénolé (1636); La Villemarqué, Barzaz Breiz, "The Submersion of the City of Ys"
(1839); Émile Souvestre, Le Foyer breton (1844); Paul Sébillot on the Breton mari-morgans (1880s); Paracelsus
(undines). **Care:** St Guénolé (a Christian saint) is not used as a character.*

**Hazard: the tide.** The sea comes in through the open gates in bands across the city (the flood bands, already in
the game).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Undines | Water casters | Paracelsus |
| Mari-morgans | Sea-fairies who lure and drown sailors | Sébillot (Breton folklore) |
| The drowned of Ys | Slow chasers | Barzaz Breiz (the drowned city) |

| Time | Boss (suggested except Dahut) | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Morvarc'h** | King Gradlon's sea-horse that gallops over the waves; charges along the tide bands | Le Grand; Souvestre (Gradlon's horse) |
| 20:00 | **The Ankou** | The Breton figure of death with his creaking cart; whoever hears the axle is marked | Anatole Le Braz, La Légende de la mort (1893) |
| 30:00 | **Dahut** (the design's) | The princess who stole the key and opened the sea gates; she opens gates round the arena, and becomes a mari-morgan in her last phase | Le Grand; Barzaz Breiz |

## Realm 16: Cloud-Cuckoo-Land (air)
*Sources: Aristophanes, The Birds (414 BC; the birds' city in the air); the One Thousand and One Nights (the Roc);
Paracelsus (sylphs). Only the old texts, never later versions.*

**Hazard: high winds.** Strong winds push Heroes and monsters (the weather push, already in the game), with gusts.

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sylphs | Fast air spirits | Paracelsus |
| The birds' army | Flocks of owls, crows and jays (the Birds' chorus) | Aristophanes, The Birds |
| Storm gusts | Whirling flyers | The Birds (the city between earth and heaven) |

| Time | Boss (suggested except the Roc) | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Hoopoe** | King of the birds (Tereus, turned into a hoopoe); calls his flocks in waves | The Birds |
| 20:00 | **Iris** | The gods' messenger who flies into the birds' city; darts across in rainbow streaks | The Birds (Iris is caught at the walls) |
| 30:00 | **The Roc** (the design's) | The bird so vast it hides the sun; drops boulders, its wingbeats knock Heroes back | The Thousand and One Nights (Sindbad's voyages) |

## Heroes
The design gives the realms no Heroes. **Suggestion:** none with Pack 4; the next Heroes come with the Act IV extras
(Hou Yi from the Realm of the Ten Suns). Or, if you'd like Heroes here, two from the design's post-launch list fit the
Norse realms: **Sigurd** (Gram; dragonslayer, bonus against bosses) and **Brynhild** (Valkyrie spear; revives allies
faster in co-op).
