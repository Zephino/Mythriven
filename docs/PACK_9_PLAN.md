# Pack 9 plan: Gears and Plunder

Status: **decided by Claude with the owner's trust** (10/31/2026: "i trust you"; "program all 9 packs 1 at a time";
11/01/2026: "go ahead" for Pack 9). The design (`GAME_DESIGN.md`: Fantasy realms) names each realm, its source and one
boss idea; everything else was chosen by Claude from old public-domain texts only, never their modern adaptations (the
design's note on Libertalia and the Nome King). The owner can change or veto any of it. Lemnos built and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.06); the Goblin Market (00.03.07); Libertalia (00.03.08); the Hollow Hills (00.03.09). **All four realms are built.**

Realms work like Packs 5-8: endless map, a hazard, monster families, three bosses, no local gods.

## How it is built
- One realm at a time: Lemnos, the Goblin Market, Libertalia, the Hollow Hills. Then two secrets per realm.
- **Heading:** "Pack 9: Gears and Plunder".
- **Unlock (Claude's choice):** each realm opens from a Pack 8 30:00 boss: Asterion → Lemnos, Vortigern → the Goblin
  Market, the Warlord → Libertalia, Útgarða-Loki → the Hollow Hills.
- **Difficulty:** as Packs 4-8 (+60% enemy HP, +35% spawns, +30% damage).
- **Heroes:** none.
- **Care:** no real historical pirates are characters (Libertalia's Captain Misson is a figure of the 1724 book); the
  Lemnian women and the Kabeiroi (a mystery cult) are left out; the Nome King is drawn from Baum's book only.

## Realm 33: Lemnos (the forge-island of automatons): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.06)**
*Sources: Homer, Iliad 1.590-594 (Hephaestus falls on Lemnos) and 18.417-420 (his golden handmaidens); Odyssey 7.91-94
(the gold and silver dogs Hephaestus made for Alcinous, "immortal and ageless"); Apollodorus, Library 1.9.23 (the
bronze-footed, fire-breathing bulls of Aeëtes, Hephaestus' work) and 1.9.26 (Talos); Apollonius of Rhodes,
Argonautica 4.1638-1688 (Talos).*

**Hazard: the smoke of the forge.** Smoke and cinders from Hephaestus' forge roll over the island, hiding the view and
Burning whoever is caught in them (the weather veil, with Burn).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bronze automatons | Clockwork men, chasers | Iliad 18 (Hephaestus' self-moving works) |
| Golden handmaidens | Casters | Iliad 18.417-420 |
| Bronze bulls | Bombers (already in the game: the khalkotauroi) | Apollodorus 1.9.23 |
| Bronze sparks | Swarms (already in the game) | the forge |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Bulls of Aeëtes** | Hephaestus' bronze-footed bulls breathe fire: a cone of flame in front of them leaves the ground burning |
| 20:00 | **The Gold and Silver Hounds** | Alcinous' immortal watchdogs take turns: the gold hound shrugs off Fire and fears Lightning, the silver one the reverse; they change every few seconds |
| 30:00 | **Talos** (the design's) | The bronze giant who guarded Crete. He hurls boulders at the Heroes, and every so often heats himself red-hot, burning whoever is near; at the end of it the nail in his ankle shows and he takes far more damage |

## Realm 34: the Goblin Market: **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.07)**
*Source: Christina Rossetti, Goblin Market (1862): the goblin merchant-men ("one had a cat's face, one whisked a tail,
one tramped at a rat's pace, one crawled like a snail, one like a wombat prowled obtuse and furry, one like a ratel
tumbled hurry skurry"), their cry "Come buy, come buy", and Lizzie, who would not open her mouth.*

**Hazard: the goblins' fruit.** Goblins pelt the Heroes with their fruit; the juice splashes and clings (telegraphed
splashes that slow, the flood bands in fruit colours).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Goblin men | Chasers | Rossetti |
| Rat-paced goblins | Swarms | Rossetti ("one tramped at a rat's pace") |
| Night bats | Flyers (already in the game) | the twilight glen |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Ratel Goblin** | "Tumbled hurry skurry": it rolls about the arena at speed in sudden, wild directions |
| 20:00 | **The Snail Goblin** | "Crawled like a snail": very slow and very tough, leaving a trail of slime that slows |
| 30:00 | **The Goblin Merchant-Men** (the design's) | They crowd round a Hero, crying "Come buy!": every so often a ring of goblins springs up round a Hero and closes in; break out of it |

## Realm 35: Libertalia (the pirate haven): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.08)**
*Sources: A General History of the Pyrates (1724), "Of Captain Misson" (Libertalia on Madagascar); Erik Pontoppidan,
The Natural History of Norway (1752-53: the Kraken, so vast it is mistaken for islands, and the great sea serpent).*

**Hazard: cannon fire.** Ships in the bay fire on the shore: telegraphed cannon-shots (the strikes, in smoke and iron).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buccaneers | Cutlass chasers | the General History |
| Musketeers | Casters | the General History |
| Gulls | Swarms | the shore |
| Drowned sailors | Brutes (already in the game: the drowned of Ys) | the Kraken's victims |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Sea Serpent** | Pontoppidan's great sea serpent rises in a line of humps across the arena (telegraphed), then sinks again |
| 20:00 | **Captain Misson** | The founder of Libertalia. His ship fires broadsides: rows of cannon-balls cross the arena from one side, port and starboard by turns |
| 30:00 | **The Kraken** (the design's) | Its arms rise and slam down on marked spots round the Heroes, and when it sinks it makes a whirlpool that drags every Hero toward it |

## Realm 36: the Hollow Hills (the gnomes' earth realm): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.09)**
*Sources: Paracelsus, Liber de Nymphis (1566: the gnomes, spirits of earth, who move through rock as we move through
air); British fairy-hill folklore (time runs differently under the hill); L. Frank Baum, Ozma of Oz (1907; the book,
not later films): the Nome King and his underground kingdom, the Giant with the Hammer, the Nome chamberlain Kaliko,
and the Nomes' terror of eggs.*

**Hazard: the hill's time.** Under the hill time runs strangely; while the gem-glitter glamour lies thick, the Heroes are
slowed (the weather veil, with Chill).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nomes | Rock-folk, chasers | Ozma of Oz |
| Gnomes | Earth spirits, casters | Paracelsus |
| Bats | Flyers (already in the game) | the caverns |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Kaliko** | The Nome King's chamberlain, who knows every tunnel: he pops up out of one of several holes, attacks, and drops down again; he can only be hurt above ground |
| 20:00 | **The Giant with the Hammer** | The iron giant who pounds the road to the Nome King's kingdom without stopping: his hammer falls on a line in front of him in a steady beat; pass between the blows |
| 30:00 | **The Nome King** (the design's; Baum's book) | He turns Heroes into ornaments for a moment (they can't move). But Nomes fear eggs: eggs appear, and a Hero who takes one terrifies him (he is stunned and takes extra damage) |
