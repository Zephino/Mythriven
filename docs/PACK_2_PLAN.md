# Pack 2 plan: Deeper Myths

Status: **approved by the owner** (10/31/2026): "plan looks great"; the Hero unlocks as suggested; the two unsourced
monsters replaced with sourced ones (below; the owner was happy for Claude to name them); Pack 2 harder than Pack 1, the
numbers left to Claude's judgement (enemy HP +20%, spawns +15%, damage +10% at the same minute, all settings). Built one
level at a time in this order: the Underworld, Jotunheim, Avalon's Shadow, the Land Between the Rivers.

Owner decisions so far (10/31/2026): Pack 1 is working and fun, so Pack 2 starts; one level at a time; each level
unlocks from its "sister" Pack 1 level; three new Heroes (Orpheus, Gilgamesh, Beowulf); this plan first.

Everything follows the naming and cultural rules in `GAME_DESIGN.md`: names and stories come from the old sources listed
(no modern adaptations), each piece of lore records its source, and nothing is invented. Where the design table already
names something (bosses, gods, hazards), this plan keeps it; anything new is marked **(suggestion)** for the owner to
approve or change.

## Level 5: The Underworld (Greek), built first: **done and released 10/31/2026 (00.02.53–00.02.59)**

**Look:** the grey fields of Asphodel, the black river Styx with Charon's ferry landing, cracked chasms down to Tartarus,
pale poplars and cypress, dim torchlight. Colors: ash grey, deep violet, cold green glow on the rivers.
*Sources: Homer, Odyssey 11 (the land of the dead, the asphodel meadow); Hesiod, Theogony 720–819 (Tartarus).*

**Hazard: rivers of the dead and collapsing chasms.** Bands of river slow and slowly drain HP while you stand in them;
cracks glow, then a chasm opens for a few seconds (telegraphed, like the Pack 1 hazards).

**Monster families (4) and elites**
| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shades (already in the game) | Swarms of the restless dead | Odyssey 11 |
| Furies-kin flyers | Fast winged pursuers that dive at you | Aeschylus, Eumenides (the Erinyes); shown as nameless winged hunters, not the three named Furies |
| Skeletal hoplites | Shielded soldiers; block from the front | The armed dead of the Odyssey 11; the "spartoi" style already in the game, in bronze |
| Stymphalian birds | Flocks that shoot bronze feathers | Apollodorus, Library 2.5.6; Apollonius, Argonautica 2 |

**Bosses (from the design)**
| Time | Boss | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Cerberus** | Three heads act on their own (fire, bite, howl); destroy them one at a time | Hesiod, Theogony 311; Apollodorus 2.5.12 |
| 20:00 | **Thanatos** | Marks a player for death with a countdown; hit him enough to cancel it. Special lines for Heracles, who wrestled him | Euripides, Alcestis |
| 30:00 | **Typhon** | A hundred serpent heads fire volleys from all sides; storms rotate the arena | Hesiod, Theogony 820–868 |

**Local gods (from the design)**
| God | Element | How you get them | Summon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Hecate | Dark | Peaceful shrine | **Three Ways:** three crossroads portals fling enemies away |
| Persephone | Earth | Trial: Endurance | **Return of Spring:** heals and blooms flowers that root enemies |

Blessings and trigger numbers are designed when the level is built, in line with the Pack 1 gods.

**Flora and critters (suggestion):** black poplars, cypress, asphodel clumps; ravens, bats, ghostly moths.

## Level 6: Jotunheim (Norse): **done and released 10/31/2026 (00.02.60–00.02.63)**

**Look:** high mountain passes, giant-sized ruined halls and steps, blue glaciers, pine forest.
*Sources: Snorri, Prose Edda (Gylfaginning; Skáldskaparmál).*

**Hazard: rockslides.** Boulders tumble down from the cliff edges in telegraphed lines.

**Monster families**
| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frost thursar (already in the game) | Hulking frost giants | Prose Edda |
| Trolls (already in the game) | Tough brutes | Norse folklore |
| Giant wolves | Fast packs; the wolf-kin of the giants | Gylfaginning (the wolves born of a giantess in Iron-wood) |
| Mountain giants (*bergrisar*) | Hurl rocks from a distance (fits the rockslide passes). Replaces "ice wisps", which had no source (owner, 10/31/2026) | Snorri, Prose Edda: Gylfaginning (the *bergrisi*, hill giants) |

**Bosses (from the design)**
| Time | Boss | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Hrungnir** | Stone heart and shield: armored until hit by Lightning | Skáldskaparmál (Thor's duel with Hrungnir) |
| 20:00 | **Thrym** | Steals one of your weapons for 20 s, as he stole Thor's hammer; win it back from his hoard | Þrymskviða (Poetic Edda) |
| 30:00 | **Ymir** | Three frost shapes, only the real one casts a shadow; "Endless Winter" freezes the floor except safe circles | Gylfaginning |

**Local gods (from the design)**
| God | Element | How you get them | Summon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Odin | Wind | **Sacrifice:** bind by giving up 30% Max HP (his self-sacrifice for wisdom) | **Gungnir:** a spear that never misses strikes the strongest enemies; ravens reveal weaknesses |
| Freyja | Holy | Trial: Chase | **Falcon Cloak:** invulnerable flight; fallen enemies briefly rise to fight for you |

**Flora and critters (suggestion):** spruce and pine, lichened boulders; ravens, mountain goats, eagles.

## Level 7: Avalon's Shadow (Welsh / Arthurian): **done and released 10/31/2026 (00.02.65)**

**Look:** ruined castles, enchanted forest, a misty lake with reeds.
*Sources: the Mabinogion (Pwyll; Culhwch and Olwen); Sir Gawain and the Green Knight; Malory, Le Morte d'Arthur.*

**Hazard: enchanted brambles.** Thorny brambles grow across paths and block them for a while, then wither.

**Monster families**
| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black knights | Armored riders who charge | Malory, Le Morte d'Arthur |
| Enchanted hounds | Fast hunting packs | Pwyll, Prince of Dyfed (hunting hounds); kept separate from Arawn's summon |
| Afanc | Lake beasts that lunge from the water | Welsh tradition (the afanc of the lake) |
| Will-o'-wisps | Lights that lure you toward hazards | British folklore |

**Bosses (from the design)**
| Time | Boss | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 20:00 | **Mordred** | His host of knights: −50% damage to him while they live; special lines for King Arthur | Malory, Le Morte d'Arthur |
| 30:00 | **Twrch Trwyth** | Charges across the whole map with his piglets; treasures between his ears drop when hit | Culhwch and Olwen |

**Local gods (from the design)**
| God | Element | How you get them | Summon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

**Flora and critters (suggestion):** oak, yew, hawthorn, reeds; owls, deer, swans on the lake.

## Level 8: The Land Between the Rivers (Mesopotamian)

**Look:** ziggurats of mud brick, the Cedar Forest, flood plains with reeds and date palms.
*Sources: the Epic of Gilgamesh (Standard Babylonian version); Enuma Elish.*

**Hazard: floods.** Flood bands sweep across the map; water slows you and puts out fire.

**Monster families**
| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scorpion-men | Guardians with poison tails | Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet IX (the scorpion-people at the mountain gate) |
| Ugallu storm-demons | Lion-headed storm spirits | Babylonian protective spirits (Enuma Elish lists the *ugallu* among Tiamat's brood) |
| Raging storms (*umū dabrūtu*) | Whirling storm-beasts that spin across the plain. Replaces "dust demons", which had no source (owner, 10/31/2026) | Enuma Elish, Tablet I (among the eleven monsters Tiamat makes) |
| River serpents | Water snakes that strike from the floods | Enuma Elish (Tiamat's serpents) |

**Bosses (from the design)**
| Time | Boss | Mechanic | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Humbaba** | Seven radiances: seven shield layers stripped one by one; Shamash's winds help (Wind damage ×2) | Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet V |
| 20:00 | **The Bull of Heaven** | Each stomp opens cracks that stay as hazards | Epic of Gilgamesh, Tablet VI |
| 30:00 | **Tiamat** | Spawns her brood of monsters; flood waves. **Original design**, not D&D's five-headed dragon | Enuma Elish |

**Local gods (from the design)**
| God | Element | How you get them | Summon |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enki | Water | Peaceful shrine | **Waters of the Abzu:** a freshwater flood heals players and Soaks enemies |
| Shamash | Holy | Peaceful shrine | **Thirteen Winds:** winds pin every enemy in place (as he helped Gilgamesh against Humbaba) |

**Flora and critters (suggestion):** cedars, date palms, reed beds; ibis, herons, wild boar.

## New Heroes

All three are on the design's post-launch Hero list. Stats and kits below are **suggestions** in line with the Pack 1
Heroes, for the owner to approve.

### Orpheus (Greek): unlocked in the Underworld
- **Unlock (suggestion):** survive 20:00 in the Underworld (he went down to bring back Eurydice).
- **Stats (suggestion):** HP 95 · Speed 100 · Armor 0 · Song-bard.
- **Signature weapon: the Lyre.** Rings of sound pulse outward from him.
- **Legend Trait (from the design):** his song charms nearby enemies to fight each other for a moment.
- **Passive (suggestion):** *Outsinging the Sirens:* allies near him attack 10% faster.
- *Sources: Ovid, Metamorphoses 10–11; Virgil, Georgics 4; Apollonius, Argonautica 4 (he drowns out the Sirens).*

### Beowulf (Geatish / Old English): unlocked in Jotunheim
- **Unlock (suggestion):** defeat 100 giants and trolls in Jotunheim. Beowulf isn't from Norse myth, but he is the great
  slayer of monsters and giant-kin (Grendel) in the old Germanic world.
- **Stats (suggestion):** HP 135 · Speed 95 · Armor 1 · Grappler.
- **Signature weapon: bare hands**, then the **giant-made sword** from the monsters' hall at weapon level 8.
- **Legend Trait (from the design):** *Grip:* grabs elites and throws them.
- **Passive (suggestion):** *The strength of thirty men:* hits knock enemies further back.
- *Sources: Beowulf (the Old English poem): the hand-grip of thirty men; the giant sword in the mere.*

### Gilgamesh (Mesopotamian): unlocked in the Land Between the Rivers
- **Unlock (suggestion):** defeat Humbaba (the Cedar Forest's guardian, whom he and Enkidu fought).
- **Stats (suggestion):** HP 140 · Speed 90 · Armor 2 · King-warrior.
- **Signature weapon: the axe.**
- **Legend Trait (from the design):** *Enkidu fights beside him* as a companion.
- **Passive (suggestion):** *Two-thirds divine:* +10% Max HP and Might.
- *Source: Epic of Gilgamesh. Drawn from the ancient reliefs and the epic, **never** from Fate/Grand Order's golden-armored
  design (named in the design's IP rules).*
