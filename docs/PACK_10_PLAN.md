# Pack 10 plan: The Heavens

Status: **decided by Claude with the owner's trust** (10/31/2026: "i trust you"; "program all 9 packs 1 at a time";
11/01/2026: "go ahead" for Pack 10). The design (`GAME_DESIGN.md`: Fantasy realms) names each realm, its source and one
boss idea; everything else was chosen by Claude from old public-domain texts only. The owner can change or veto any of
it. Hyperborea built and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.11); the Empyrean (00.03.12); the Glass Mountain (00.03.13).

Realms work like Packs 5-9: endless map, a hazard, monster families, three bosses, no local gods.

## How it is built
- One realm at a time: Hyperborea, the Empyrean, the Glass Mountain, Demos Oneiron. Then two secrets per realm.
- **Heading:** "Pack 10: The Heavens".
- **Unlock (Claude's choice):** each realm opens from a Pack 9 30:00 boss: Talos → Hyperborea, the Kraken → the
  Empyrean, the Goblin Merchant-Men → the Glass Mountain, the Nome King → Demos Oneiron.
- **Difficulty:** as Packs 4-9 (+60% enemy HP, +35% spawns, +30% damage).
- **Heroes:** none.
- **Care:** the Empyrean is the old astronomers' sky of spheres and constellations (Aratus, Ptolemy, Hyginus); Dante's
  heaven, being religious, is left out. The Snow Queen is Andersen's (1844), not any film's. The Hyperboreans
  themselves are a blessed, peaceful people in the old texts, so they are not enemies.

## Realm 37: Hyperborea (beyond the north wind): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.11)**
*Sources: Herodotus, Histories 4.13-36 (the Hyperboreans; the one-eyed Arimaspians who steal gold from the griffins;
the air of the north "full of feathers"; the men who sleep six months of the year, 4.25); Pindar, Pythian 10.*

**Hazard: the air full of feathers.** Herodotus' "feathers" fill the air (snow): the view closes in.

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arimaspians | One-eyed raiders, chasers | Herodotus 4.13, 4.27 |
| Young griffins | Flyers | Herodotus 3.116, 4.13 |
| Frost wisps | Casters (already in the game) | the north |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Arimaspian Chieftain** | The one-eyed raiders steal the griffins' gold: every blow he lands steals some of your run gold, and he drops it all when his guard breaks |
| 20:00 | **The Six-Month Sleeper** | One of the northern men who sleep half the year: awake he fights hard; asleep he heals, but takes double damage |
| 30:00 | **The Griffin Matriarch** (the design's) | She guards her golden nest: on it she heals and takes half damage; then she leaves it to swoop on a Hero from the sky |

## Realm 38: the Empyrean (the star realm): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.12)**
*Sources: Aratus, Phaenomena (3rd c. BC); Ptolemy, Almagest (the constellations); Hyginus, De Astronomia 2.21, 2.26,
2.34; Ovid, Metamorphoses 2.401-530 (Callisto, the Great Bear) and Fasti 5.537-544 (the Scorpion sent against Orion);
Apollodorus 1.4.3-5 (Orion, blinded, walks toward the sunrise).*

**Hazard: falling stars.** Stars fall out of the sky (telegraphed strikes, pale and bright).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Star hounds | Orion's dogs among the stars, chasers | Aratus; Hyginus 2.35-36 |
| Star motes | Swarms of small lights | the night sky |
| Comets | Flyers | Aratus |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Scorpion** | Sent by Earth to kill Orion. Its tail strikes at a Hero (marked), and the sting Poisons and leaves a pool of venom |
| 20:00 | **The Great Bear** | Callisto, set in the sky; the Bear never sets: she circles the arena without stopping, swiping whoever is near her path |
| 30:00 | **Orion** (the design's) | The giant hunter calls his hounds; every so often he is struck blind and lashes about wildly at random, taking more damage until his sight returns |

## Realm 39: the Glass Mountain (the crystal realm): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.03.13)**
*Sources: the Brothers Grimm, "The Seven Ravens" (KHM 25) and "Old Rinkrank" (KHM 196): the glass mountain; Hans
Christian Andersen, "The Snow Queen" (1844; not any film): the white bees, the snowflake army, the frozen lake, the
pieces of ice Kai must fit into the word "Eternity".*

**Hazard: falling icicles on the glass slopes.** Icicles crash down (telegraphed strikes that Chill), and the glass
slopes are slippery (the slide patches).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

| Snowflake guards | The Snow Queen's living snowflakes, chasers | Andersen, 7th story |
| Frost wisps | Casters (already in the game) | |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Seven Ravens** | The brothers turned into ravens. Every so often the boss scatters into seven ravens (it can't be hurt); each raven killed hurts it, and they gather again |

## Realm 40: Demos Oneiron (the land of dreams)
*Sources: Homer, Odyssey 24.12 (the land of dreams, past the White Rock) and 19.560-569 (the gates of horn and ivory);
Ovid, Metamorphoses 11.583-649 (the House of Sleep; the dream-shapers Morpheus, Phobetor and Phantasos).*

**Hazard: the dream lights.** Lights drift through the dream and draw the monsters toward them (the wandering lights,
in dream colours).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dreams | Swarms, "as many as leaves in the woods" | Ovid 11.613-615 |
| Nightmare beasts | Chasers | Ovid 11.637-640 (Phobetor's beasts) |
| Night bats | Flyers (already in the game) | |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Phantasos** | The dream-shaper of things: he becomes a rock (still, hard to hurt) or water (fast, slipping away), by turns |
| 20:00 | **Morpheus** | The dream-shaper of men, who imitates everything: a share of every blow struck at him is turned back on whoever struck it |
| 30:00 | **Phobetor** (the design's) | The bringer of nightmares, called Icelus by the gods: he takes the shape of a beast (charges), a bird (flies out of reach and strikes from above) and a serpent (a ring of venom), by turns |
