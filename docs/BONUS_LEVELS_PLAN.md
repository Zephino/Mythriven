# Bonus levels plan: the Realm of the Ten Suns and the Crossroads

Status: **decided by Claude with the owner's trust** (10/31/2026: "i trust you"). Everything below comes from
`GAME_DESIGN.md` (Act IV levels, Acts II-IV bosses, Post-launch Heroes, The Crossroads); where the design left a
choice open, Claude chose and says so. The owner can change any of it before or after it is built.

## Order
1. **The Realm of the Ten Suns** (level 53), with the Hero **Hou Yi**.
2. **The Crossroads** (level 54), the finale, with **the Unweaver**.

## Level 53: the Realm of the Ten Suns (Chinese legend): **done and released 10/31/2026 (00.02.82)**
*Sources: the Huainanzi (c. 139 BC: Yi shoots down nine of the ten suns); the Shanhaijing; Journey to the West
(Baigujing, the White Bone Demon); Chinese New Year folklore (Nian). Chinese folk deities are Tier B, so the level has
no local gods: shrines come from every pantheon. Sun Wukong stays out (Tier B).*

- **Look (design):** scorched terraced hills, rivers, pagodas.
- **Hazard (design):** extreme heat bands: bands of scorching heat cross the map; stand in shade (the shadow of trees,
  pagodas and rocks) or be burned. *(As built: the heat bands burn whoever stands in them; step out of the band to be
  safe. Shade from trees was left out for now.)*
- **Monsters (design):** bone demons, fox spirits, fire beasts.
- **Bosses (design):**
  | Time | Boss | Mechanic |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 10:00 | **Nian** | Frightened by noise and red: Lightning and Fire hits stun it briefly |
  | 20:00 | **Baigujing**, the White Bone Demon | Takes three disguises in turn; striking the wrong one heals her |
  | 30:00 | **The Ten Suns** | Ten suns blaze overhead; shoot down nine, but if the last sun falls the world goes dark |

  *(As built: the fight is won the moment the ninth sun falls, so the last sun always stays in the sky, as in the
  legend; the "world goes dark" outcome can't happen.)*
- **Hero (design): Hou Yi**, unlocked by defeating the Ten Suns. Great bow; Legend Trait "shoots down suns": bonus
  damage to flyers and bosses. Like the other bow Heroes, he shoots a fan of 2 weaker arrows (owner rule 00.02.50).
- **Unlock (Claude's choice):** beat the Roc, Cloud-Cuckoo-Land's 30:00 boss, so the bonus levels follow Pack 4.
- **Difficulty (Claude's choice):** as Pack 4 (+60% HP, +35% spawns, +30% damage).

## Level 54: the Crossroads (the finale): **done and released 10/31/2026 (00.02.83)**
*The Crossroads, the Unweaving and the Unweaver are the game's own invented names, already in the design.*

- **Look (design):** every region's terrain blended and fraying: Greek columns beside Norse runestones, obelisks and
  stone circles.
- **Hazard (design):** rotates every Age between all the hazards of the other levels.
- **Monsters (design):** all families.
- **Shrines (design):** all pantheons.
- **Bosses (design):** 10:00 and 20:00 are random bosses from any region; 30:00 is **the Unweaver**, an original chaos
  being who steals the patterns of every boss you have beaten and swallows and unweaves gods, two at once.
- **Unlock (design):** defeat the 30:00 boss in at least 8 levels.
- **Difficulty (Claude's choice):** the hardest: +80% HP, +45% spawns, +40% damage.

## Afterwards (as with the packs)
Boss achievements, two secrets each, banter, lore check rows, and a refreshed sprite sheet.

*(As built, 00.02.83: the hazard takes the next of twelve levels' hazards each time it comes; the wave table mixes
monsters from every land and changes every couple of minutes; fairy-ring style portals carry Heroes across the
blended land; the 10:00 and 20:00 bosses are drawn from the other levels when the run starts; the Unweaver takes one
stolen boss's attacks for each of its phases (from bosses you have beaten, else any) and unweaves both god meters of
every Hero every 15 s.)*

*(Secrets, 00.02.84: **Firecrackers** (frighten Nian 5 times, a red-lantern aura); **the Nine Crows** (shoot down the
nine suns within 120 s, the Sun-crow Feather passive: +2% crit per level); **the Last Thread** (beat the Unweaver
before its fourth unweaving, the Loose Thread passive: weapon cooldowns 3% shorter per level); **Nothing to Steal**
(beat the Unweaver without it ever unweaving a full god meter of yours, a many-coloured thread aura). Baigujing has no
secret, so each bonus level has two.)*
