# Pack 7 plan: The Wild Green

Status: **decided by Claude with the owner's trust** (10/31/2026: "i trust you"; "program all 9 packs 1 at a time";
11/01/2026: "go ahead" for Pack 7). The design (`GAME_DESIGN.md`: Fantasy realms) names each realm, its source and one
boss idea; everything else was chosen by Claude from old public-domain texts only. Alfheim built and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.96); Elfame (00.02.97); Arcadia (00.02.98). The owner can change or veto any of
it.

Realms work like Packs 5 and 6: endless map, a hazard, monster families, three bosses, no local gods.

## How it is built
- One realm at a time: Alfheim, Elfame, Arcadia, Lerna. Then two secrets per realm.
- **Heading:** "Pack 7: The Wild Green".
- **Unlock (Claude's choice):** each realm opens from a Pack 6 30:00 boss: Andvari → Alfheim, Alberich → Elfame, the
  Spore Mother → Arcadia, the Ant Queen → Lerna.
- **Difficulty:** as Packs 4-6 (+60% enemy HP, +35% spawns, +30% damage).
- **Heroes:** none.

## Realm 25: Alfheim (the elves): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.96)**
*Sources: the Poetic Edda, Grímnismál 5 (Álfheimr, given to Freyr) and Völundarkviða (Völundr, "prince of elves", who
forged himself wings and flew from his captor); Snorri, Gylfaginning 17 (the light elves, fairer than the sun); the
Old English charm "Wið færstice" (10th c.: elf-shot, sudden pains from elves' arrows); J. G. Herder, "Erlkönigs
Tochter" (1778, from the Danish ballad of Sir Oluf); Goethe, "Erlkönig" (1782).* Freyr, a god, is not a boss.

**Hazard: elf-shot.** Unseen elves' arrows strike out of the air (telegraphed strikes, pale gold) and leave whoever
they hit Shocked (the next hit hurts more), as the old charm's "sudden stitch".

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Light elves | Casters | Gylfaginning 17 |
| Elf-knights | Chasers | the ballads of the elf-knight |
| Elf-maidens | Swarms of dancers (the Erlking's daughters) | Herder; Goethe |
| Will-o'-the-wisps | Flyers (already in the game) | folklore |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **The Erlking's Daughter** | She asks Sir Oluf to dance; he refuses, and she strikes his heart. A dance ring appears round a Hero: stay inside it until the dance ends, or her elf-shot strikes |
| 20:00 | **Völundr** | The elf-smith takes to the air on the wings he forged: while flying he can't be reached, and he drops strikes on marked spots before landing |
| 30:00 | **The Erlking** (the design's) | "And if you're not willing, I'll use force." He draws the nearest Hero toward him with his lure, and his daughters dance round him |

## Realm 26: Elfame (the Fae Court): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.97)**
*Sources: the Scottish ballads Thomas the Rhymer (Child 37) and Tam Lin (Child 39); Lady Isabel and the Elf-Knight
(Child 4); the romance of Thomas of Erceldoune (15th c.).* The ballads' "teind to hell" is left out.

**Hazard: the elfin glamour.** A glittering glamour hides the view, and fairy rings carry whoever steps in one across
the land (the weather veil with petals, and the teleport rings).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fairy riders | The fairy rade's riders, fast chasers | Tam Lin (the rade at Halloween) |
| Redcaps | Chasers (already in the game) | Border folklore |
| The sluagh | Swarms and flyers (already in the game) | Scottish folklore |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Tam Lin** | The knight the fairies stole. Janet held him while they turned him into an adder, a bear, a lion and burning iron. He changes shape and burns whoever is close, but a Hero who holds on beside him through a whole change frees him for a while (his guard breaks) |
| 20:00 | **The Elf-Knight** | He blows his horn and draws the Heroes toward him; but Lady Isabel lulled him to sleep: if no one hurts him for a few seconds he dozes off and takes extra damage |
| 30:00 | **The Queen of Elfame** (the design's) | The queen who took Thomas the Rhymer for seven years and warned him not to eat the fruit of her garden. Fruit appears in the arena: eating it heals you, but it binds your tongue (both god meters empty) |

## Realm 27: Arcadia (the beastfolk wilds): **done and released 11/01/2026 (00.02.98)**
*Sources: Ovid, Metamorphoses 1.163-243 (Lycaon, turned into a wolf when Zeus struck his house); Apollodorus,
Library 2.5.3-4 (the Ceryneian Hind; the Erymanthian Boar, driven into the snow; the centaurs of Pholoe); Pausanias,
Book 8 (Arcadia).*

**Hazard: the stampede.** Herds of wild beasts stampede across the hills in bands (the flood bands, in earth colours),
slowing whoever is caught in them.

| Family | What it does | Source |

| Satyrs | Chasers (already in the game) | Arcadian myth |
| Wolves | Packs (already in the game) | Ovid (Lycaon) |
| Stymphalian birds | Swarms and archers (already in the game) | Apollodorus 2.5.6 (the Stymphalian lake is in Arcadia) |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |

## Realm 28: Lerna (the serpent-marsh of the Spartoi)
*Sources: Hesiod, Theogony 313-318 (the Hydra of Lerna); Apollodorus, Library 2.5.2 (the Hydra's nine heads, the
immortal head buried under a rock; Iolaus searing the necks; the crab Hera sent) and 3.4.1 (Cadmus sows the dragon's
teeth; the Spartoi fight each other when he throws a stone among them; Echion is one of the five who survive);
Pausanias 2.37 (Lerna's bottomless lake, a way down to the underworld).*

**Hazard: the rising swamp.** Bands of stagnant water spread over the marsh, slowing and Poisoning whoever they reach
(the flood bands, with Poison).

| Family | What it does | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Spartoi | Sown warriors, chasers; the brutes are already in the game | Apollodorus 3.4.1 |
| Water snakes | Swarms (the river serpents, already in the game) | the marsh |
| Shades | Flyers (already in the game), up from the lake | Pausanias 2.37 |

| Time | Boss | Mechanic |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10:00 | **Karkinos** | The giant crab Hera sent against Heracles. Its shell turns blows from the front; strike it from the side or behind |
| 20:00 | **Echion** | One of the sown men. He raises Spartoi from the ground; a stone lies in the arena, and a Hero who takes it throws it among them, so they fight each other for a while |
| 30:00 | **The Lernaean Hydra** (the design's) | Its heads grow back, more each time (more attacks); Fire sears a neck and keeps a head from growing. Its last head is immortal: at the end it can't die until a Hero buries the head under the rock that appears |
