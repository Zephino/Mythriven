# MYTHRIVEN

**Every legend has a breaking point.**

*A top-down survival game where heroes from the world's myths and legends make their last stand against an endless tide of monsters.*

## ▶ [Play Mythriven in your browser](https://zephino.github.io/Mythriven/)

**https://zephino.github.io/Mythriven/**. There's nothing to install. It runs on PC, phone and tablet, and you can install it as an app from **Settings**.

> Mythriven is still being made. Things change often, and some art and numbers are not final.

---

## How to play, in one minute

1. Press **Play**, choose a **level**, then choose a **Hero**.
2. **Move.** Your weapons attack by themselves. All you do is steer.
3. **Pick up the glowing orbs** monsters drop (experience). Each time you level up, **choose one card**: a new weapon, a new passive, or an upgrade.
4. **Find the shrines** and win **gods** to your side, then **call them** when their meter is full.
5. **Survive.** Big bosses arrive at **10:00, 20:00 and 30:00**. Every run ends sooner or later, but the **gold** you earn is kept for upgrades between runs.

> **The golden rule: keep moving.** Monsters don't pile on top of each other and you have no moment of safety after being hit, so standing still in a crowd is deadly.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad | Phone / tablet |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move (8 directions) | WASD or arrow keys, or hold the left mouse button and drag | Left stick or D-pad | Put a finger down anywhere and drag (a joystick appears) |
| Call god 1 | **Q** | LB | God button (bottom right) |
| Call god 2 | **E** | RB | God button |
| Call god 3 (only if you have a third god) | **F** | — | God button |
| Divine Resonance (joint attack) | **R**, or Q and E together | LB and RB together | **Resonance!** button |
| Open a saved level-up | **Tab** | Y / Triangle | Tap the level badge |
| Pause | **Esc** | Start | Pause button |

- **You never aim.** Each weapon picks its own target: the nearest enemy, the direction you're moving, a circle around you, and so on. Bosses and elite monsters are hit first when they're close.
- **Phone players:** in **Settings → Controls** you can make the touch buttons bigger or smaller, and keep the joystick to the left or right half of the screen. The god buttons move to the other side.

## A run, from start to finish

### The clock
A run gets harder as time passes, in four **Ages**:

| Time | Age | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 – 10:00 | **Dusk** | A gentle start. Elite monsters begin at 5:00. |
| 10:00 – 20:00 | **Night** | First boss at 10:00. More and tougher monsters. |
| 20:00 – 30:00 | **Deep Night** | Second boss at 20:00. Elites get two special powers. |
| 30:00 on | **The Unraveling** | Third boss at 30:00. Monsters can now Burn, Chill or Poison you. |

Monsters get steadily tougher until 30:00. Each level also has its own **hazard** (a thunderstorm, a blizzard, a sandstorm, falling rocks, and so on). It always shows a warning on the ground first, so watch your feet.

### Bosses
At 10:00, 20:00 and 30:00 a boss arrives. All ordinary monsters on screen burst into experience, and a **ring** closes in around you. Every boss has its own trick, and the **tip on the screen** tells you how to beat it, such as "circle behind him" or "stand at the bell." Beating a boss gives you:
- a big golden **chest**,
- a **Boon** (a choice of three lasting bonuses for the run),
- extra **gold**.

### The Unraveling (the end of the run)
After the 30:00 boss falls (or at 33:00), a **wall forms around you and closes in a step every 30 seconds**. When it reaches you, the run is over. Use that time to grab what you can.

### When the run ends
A run ends when you fall, when the Unraveling closes in, or when you quit. You then see a **recap** of your run with a tip, and you can post your run to the **Leaderboard**.

**Gold you keep:**
- If you **fall**, you keep **all** your gold.
- If you **quit** after 5:00, you keep **10%**. Quitting before 5:00 keeps nothing, and closing the game counts as quitting.

So fighting to the end always pays better than quitting.

## Getting stronger during a run

### Level-up cards
- Each level-up shows **3 cards** (4 if your Luck is high). Pick one.
- The game **pauses** while you choose.
- **Not sure?** Close the cards to **save the level-up for later**, then open it again with **Tab**, **Y**, or by tapping the level badge.
- **Reroll** gives you new cards. You get one free at the start and another every 5 levels. After that, rerolls cost gold.
- **Banish** (with a Banish token from the Trader) removes something from your cards for the rest of the run.

**Slots:** you can carry **5 weapons** (your Hero's own weapon counts as one), **3 spells** (Ember, Frost, Spark and the like have their own slots) and **6 passives**. Your weapons, spells and their levels are shown down the left side of the screen. Weapons go up to **level 8**, and passives to level 5. Each Hero is an **expert** with some kinds of weapons, which get an extra shot at level 8.

When everything is full and maxed, cards become **Gold** or **Heal**. The **Auto-collect** setting can pick these for you.

### Chests and evolutions
Elite monsters drop **chests**, and bosses and treasure spots drop big golden ones. Chests level up your weapons and passives.

**Evolution:** a **level-8 weapon** plus **its matching passive** turns into a much stronger weapon at the **next chest**. Every recipe you discover is written in the **Codex**.

### Elements
Weapons, spells and gods carry **elements**, and each one does something extra. There is a spell for every element: **Ember** (Fire), **Frost** (Ice), **Spark** (Lightning), **Splash** (Water) and **Stone** (Earth) from the start, and **Breeze** (Wind), **Glimmer** (Holy) and **Shade** (Dark) bought in the Hall of Legends. Spells rename as they level up (Splash → Torrent → Riptide).

| Element | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 🔥 Fire | **Burn**: damage over time |
| ❄️ Ice | **Chill**: slows; three Chills **Freeze** |
| ⚡ Lightning | **Shock**: the next hit does more |
| 💧 Water | **Soaked**: sets up combos |
| 🪨 Earth | **Poison**: stacks up to 5 times |
| 🌬️ Wind | **Gust**: knocks enemies back |
| ✨ Holy | **Smite**: breaks shields |
| 🌑 Dark | **Hex**: weaker armor and weaker attacks |

- A big yellow **"!"** means you hit a monster's **weakness** (extra damage). Small grey numbers mean it **resists** that element.
- **Combos:**
  - **Conduct!** Lightning on a Soaked monster jumps to every Soaked monster nearby.
  - **Steam Burst!** Fire on a Chilled or Frozen monster makes it explode.
  - **Shatter!** A physical hit on a Frozen monster does double damage.
  - **Spread!** Wind carries Burn, Poison or Soaked to nearby monsters.

You can look up every monster's weakness in the **Bestiary** after you've beaten enough of them.

## The gods

There are **20 gods** from many mythologies. Each gives you a **Blessing** (a lasting bonus) and a **summon** (a big attack you call with a button).

### Winning a god
- A **shrine** appears about every 3 minutes (the first at 1:30). An **arrow at the edge of the screen** points to it. Hurry, because it doesn't wait forever.
- Some shrines lead into a short **dungeon** with the god waiting in a throne room at the end.
- **Peaceful shrines:** stand in the circle for 2 seconds.
- **Trial shrines:** you must earn the god. You can step out of the ring at any time to give up safely.
  - **Duel:** beat the god in a fight within 60 seconds, without leaving the ring.
  - **Endurance:** survive 40 seconds while the god throws everything at you.
  - **Chase:** touch the fleeing god 3 times in 40 seconds (watch out for decoys).

### Using your gods
- You can hold **2 gods**, or **3** if you find the **hidden shrine** inside a hidden grove. If your slots are full, you choose which god to let go.
- Each god's **meter fills in its own way**: some by picking things up, some by taking hits, some by attacking or killing, and some over time. When it's full, press its button (**Q**, **E** or **F**). A full meter waits for you.
- **Auto-summon** (in Settings) calls a ready god for you when you're swarmed or a boss is near.
- **Divine Resonance:** some pairs of gods belong together. With both of them in your first two slots you get a bonus, and when **both meters are full** you can press **R** for a **joint attack**.
- **Infusion:** a level-8 weapon plus a god of a different element can be **infused** at the next chest, taking on that god's element and power. One infusion per weapon.

## Exploring the world

Each map goes on forever and is different every run. Look around:

| What you find | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Ancient Ruins** | Stairs down into a maze full of monsters, with a treasure vault at the end |
| **Dragon's Hoard** | Defeat 50 monsters inside its circle for a chest and gold |
| **Monster's Lair** | Three elites and their guards behind walls, for a big chest and gold |
| **Hidden Shrine** | An extra shrine, often with a rarer god |
| **Wandering Trader** | Spend your run's gold on bread (full heal), whetstones (weapon level), incense (fills a god meter), reroll and banish tokens, and rope. Prices rise each Age. |
| **Hidden Groves** | Clearings hidden in thick forest. Look for a faint glint and a way through the trees. Some hold treasure, and one in a long while holds the shrine that gives you a **third god**. |
| **Mountains** | Climb up by the handholds, or use a **rope** for a quick ladder. Mend the rope bridge to reach the far peak's **treasure cave**. Roads often lead toward mountains. |

The **compass** shows these places at the edge of the screen. They show as "?" until you get closer.

### The Hall of Legends
The Hall is where you spend gold and look back on your runs:
- **Progress:**
  - **Offerings** raise your gods' levels (up to 10) with gold.
  - **Unlocks** buy Heroes and weapons, and change your aura colour.
  - **Omens** make runs harder for more gold.
- **Lore:**
  - The **Bestiary** collects every monster you've met.
  - The **Pantheon Compendium** collects the gods and their stories.
  - The **Hall of Heroes** shows each Hero's legend and best time.
  - The **Codex** lists your evolutions, infusions and Resonances.
- **Records:**
  - **Achievements**.
  - **The Oracle**, who whispers a hint about a hidden **secret** every 5 runs.
  - The **Leaderboard**.

**Secrets:** there are over 100 of them. They're hidden until you find them, and they give new Heroes, weapons, passives, auras and Omens.

**Omens:** these open after you first reach 10:00. Turn them on before a run for more gold:

| Omen | Harder | Extra gold |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Haste | Monsters move 25% faster | +25% |
| Frailty | 30% less health | +30% |
| Horde | 50% more monsters | +40% |
| Silent Gods | God meters fill half as fast | +35% |

**Swift Mode:** beat a level's 30:00 boss to unlock a faster version of that level, with bosses at 7, 14 and 21 minutes and more gold.

## Playing with friends (online co-op)

Up to **4 players** can play together.
- **Host:** choose **Co-op → Host a game**, and share the **6-digit code**.
- **Join:** choose **Co-op → Join** and type the code. You can also browse **Public Games**, and even join a run that has already started.
- From the **pause menu**, a solo run can **invite friends** or **open to the public**.
- Everyone needs the **same version** of the game. If the host leaves, the game ends.

**How co-op works:**
- Experience and levels are shared, and players take **turns** choosing level-up cards.
- Stay close together; the game keeps you within about a screen and a half of each other.
- At 0 health you're **downed**. Stand beside a downed friend for a few seconds to bring them back. Each player can be revived twice.
- After that you become a **Wandering Shade**: you can't fight, but you still help.
- Monsters and bosses get tougher with each extra player.

## Saving your progress

- Your progress **saves by itself** on your device, and the game works offline.
- To play on more than one device, go to **Settings → Account & cloud save** and either:
  - **sign in with Google** (you choose a username, and your real name is never shown), or
  - turn on cloud saves **without an account** and use the **sync code** on your other device.
- A cloud save can be played on **one device at a time**. Another device can take over a run in progress.

## Tips

- **Keep moving.** Circle around the crowd rather than backing into a corner.
- **Grab shrines early.** Gods make a huge difference, and a god's meter waits for you once it's full.
- **Watch the ground.** Every hazard and most boss attacks are marked on the ground before they hit.
- **Hunt elites.** They drop chests, and chests are how weapons evolve.
- **Read the boss tip.** Every boss has a weakness, like hitting it from behind, ringing a bell, or standing on its hill.
- **Fall, don't quit.** Falling keeps all your gold; quitting keeps only a little.
- **Save a level-up** when none of the cards fit your build; a better choice may come later.

## About the myths

Mythriven draws on old myths, folk tales and legends from many cultures. It tells them in its own way and takes nothing from modern films, books or games. Where a belief is still practised today, the game treats it with care.
